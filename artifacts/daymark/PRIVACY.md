# Daymark privacy notice

Daymark is designed to work without an account and without a server.

- Tasks, notes, activity history, and preferences are stored locally on the
  user's device.
- Daymark does not sell personal information.
- Daymark does not include advertising or analytics.
- Notifications are optional and are scheduled locally on the device.
- Export is user-initiated and shares only the local Daymark data selected by
  the user.

Deleting the app removes its local data according to the device's normal app
storage behavior.
